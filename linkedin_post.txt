LinkedIn Post — AI Job Hunter
==============================

I see people in reels flexing "Connect Claude to Apify and automate your job search!"

Cool demo. But here's the problem:

- Claude/paid APIs = $$$$ per token
- Rate limits hit you mid-search
- Subscription required for decent usage
- One actor runs, and your credits evaporate

So I built the same thing — but free.

Stack:
- Apify (LinkedIn Jobs Scraper) — the same actor everyone uses
- Groq API — free tier, runs LLM inference fast
- ATS scoring engine — deterministic 8-category analysis (skills, experience, role alignment, education, certifications...)
- Streamlit — clean chat UI

How it works:
1. Upload your CV — any background, tech or non-tech
2. It auto-detects your skills and sends them to LinkedIn
3. Apify scrapes fresh matching jobs
4. Every job gets an ATS score (0-100) against YOUR CV — irrelevant jobs get filtered out
5. Jobs sorted by match score — highest first, with hyperlinks
6. Click "Full Analysis" on any job → full breakdown: category scores, matched skills, missing requirements, recommendations

No Claude subscription. No API bills. Same Apify actors. Free LLM.

The reels show Claude because it looks cool. But for job search automation, you don't need $20/month — you need Apify + a free LLM + smart engineering.

Stop paying for what you can build for free.
